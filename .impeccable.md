# Slop design context

## Design Context

### Users

Contributors use their preferred agent to find bounded, unclaimed open-source work, ship it on GitHub, and inspect acceptance, score and earnings. Maintainers review outcomes in their own repositories and manage project policy. Sponsors inspect funding rights and evidence before committing funds. Public readers follow the work and settlement record. The product serves both quick mobile discovery and careful desktop review.

### Brand Personality

**Direct, industrious, accountable.** The promise is **make money shipping open source**. The interface should create confidence, momentum, craftsmanship and transparency. Plain language states what happened, who decides and what comes next. No theatrical certainty, invented balances or automatic approval claims.

### Aesthetic Direction

Extend the maintainer-adopted Blackout system in PRD UX-01: coal `#0f0e0c`, cream `#f3efe6`, one functional orange `#ff5a19`, the canonical dripping-S tile, uppercase Bricolage Grotesque headings and JetBrains Mono numbers. Use a centered, compact hero and centered page layouts with the original 1180px maximum width and outer gutters. Favor compact headings, tight section spacing, dense lists and tables, and readable body text. Keep mobile touch controls usable. Full-width pages were tried and rejected. Never use side-card tabs or status panels with a colored side stripe; use plain text summaries and distinct components instead. This direction reflects the user's live-app feedback; the earlier oversized Figma vision was rejected. Light surfaces are purposeful inverse areas, not a second unapproved theme. No visual references were supplied; the repository brand and product contracts are the reference. Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, bento grids, gradients, ornamental charts and repeated rounded cards.

### Design Principles

1. **Lead with useful work.** Put actionable projects and bounded opportunities ahead of mechanism explanations. GitHub remains work, review and policy authority.
2. **Give every number a meaning.** State scope, period, denomination, funding and lifecycle. Accepted-work score, participation points, allocation weight and money are distinct.
3. **Make evidence inspectable.** Compact summaries expand to exact public records. Never expose private traces, prompts, credentials or signing material.
4. **Make the next action honest.** Humans accept work. Wallet proof establishes control only. Disabled and gated capabilities explain their dependency without simulating production success.
5. **Design for reflow and recovery.** Auto Layout, reusable variants, readable lines, 48 px touch actions, visible keyboard focus, AA contrast and separate loading, empty, stale, invalid and error states. Preserve drafts and safe return paths.

## Scope and status
